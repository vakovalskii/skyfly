# Generated rooftop materials

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-09-29. The selected PNGs
are stored in this project and served locally. Applied only to the Moscow
starting building in full rendering mode; original OSM contour and collision
geometry are preserved. Roof repeats every 4 metres, facade every 12 metres.

- `rooftop-limestone-v1.png` — roof paving, also used as a subtle bump map.
- `rooftop-building-facade-v1.png` — exterior limestone/window material.

## Final roof prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square albedo texture for a 3D game's rooftop walking surface. Primary request: beautiful realistic light grey limestone paving slabs with subtle warm mineral flecks, fine stone grain, very narrow medium grey grout lines, restrained natural weathering. Composition: orthographic straight top down, flat texture filling the entire square, an even repeating arrangement of large rectangular stone slabs, roughly 4 metres of paving in view. Lighting: entirely diffuse neutral illumination, no directional lighting and no cast shadows, suitable for live evening lighting in a game. Constraints: perfectly tileable opposite edges, consistent physical scale, no perspective, no scene objects, no people, no buildings, no decorations, no lettering, no border or watermark. Produce one square game material texture.

## Final facade prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square seamless albedo texture for the exterior facade of a Moscow rooftop building in a 3D game. Primary request: refined pale grey limestone facade with tall dark blue-grey window glazing, slender stone piers and subtle horizontal stone cornices. Composition: exactly orthographic front elevation, four floors by four identical-width window bays across the whole square, no perspective or building corners, edge-to-edge architectural wall material representing 12 by 12 metres. Window interiors mostly dark, restrained variation in curtains, elegant real stone grain and precise metal window frames. Lighting: neutral diffuse flat illumination suitable for real-time evening sunlight, no strong baked lighting, no cast shadows. Perfectly repeatable opposing edges horizontally and vertically; the border continues the wall module seamlessly. No roof, no sky, no ground, no people, no plants, no signage, no writing, no watermark.
